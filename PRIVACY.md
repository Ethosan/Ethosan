# eatentam: Privacy

eatentam is the personal site of Ethan Tam. It has no user accounts, no comments, no analytics, and no tracking. This page says what data touches the service and where it goes.

## What is collected

- **Nothing stored about you.** The site doesn't ask for your name, email, or any sign-in. There are no cookies set for tracking.
- **Server logs.** The hosting provider (Netlify / Cloudflare) keeps standard access logs (IP address, request time, page requested) for security and performance. These are kept briefly and are not used to profile you.

## Who else processes data

- **Netlify** and **Cloudflare:** hosting and CDN. Their own privacy policies apply.
- **Google Fonts:** the page's typefaces load from Google.

## What is not done

- No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cross-site tracking.
- No selling or sharing of personal data (there isn't any to sell).
- No account system.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the site will say so before it takes effect.
